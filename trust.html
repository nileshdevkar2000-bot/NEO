TRUST / CONTROL / SECURITY

Designed for environments where evidence matters.

NEO's product principles are centered on explainability, source traceability, permission-aware access and human approval. Production regulatory use requires additional validation and controls.

EVIDENCE

Source-linked outputs

Relevant AI workflows should preserve the underlying record, document or data reference used to produce an explanation.

AUTHORITY

Human approval gates

AI can prepare work. Authorized people approve, sign and release controlled outputs.

BOUNDARIES

Client isolation

Customer-specific knowledge is intended to remain isolated through customer-specific access and data controls.

AUDITABILITY

Action history

Controlled workflows should record material actions, approvals and changes for review.

SAFETY

No autonomous regulated communication

NEO's intended model does not allow the AI to independently communicate decisions to auditors, vendors or clients.

IMPROVEMENT

Versioned learning

Kaizen improvements should move through controlled product and process changes rather than uncontrolled behavior changes.

IMPORTANT

Website concept ≠ validated QMS.

This website is a prototype and product foundation. A real pharmaceutical deployment requires validated computerized-system architecture, appropriate electronic-record and electronic-signature controls, security, access control, testing, data-integrity controls, SOPs and an applicable regulatory assessment.
