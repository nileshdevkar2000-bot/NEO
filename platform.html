Intelligence across the organization. Control at every step.

NEO Workspace

NEO DESIGN RULES

Automation without surrendering accountability.

These principles should remain stable even as NEO grows beyond pharma.

01

Source before synthesis

Start with the controlled source before the generated explanation.

02

Human before release

Prepared content does not become an official communication without authorization.

03

Permission before retrieval

Department and client boundaries determine what can be accessed.

04

Trace before trust

Keep the action, source and approval history visible for relevant workflows.
